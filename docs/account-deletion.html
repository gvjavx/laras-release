Laras — Request Account Deletion

You can request that your Laras account and all associated data (learning progress, quiz results, writing/speaking submissions, settings) be permanently deleted from our server.

How to request deletion

Send an email to naufalhilmi1809@gmail.com from the email address registered to your Laras account, with the subject line "Delete my Laras account". Include your account's registered email address in the message body.

Send deletion request

What gets deleted

Your account, learning progress, quiz results, writing submissions, speaking recordings, achievements, and settings stored on the central server will be permanently deleted. This cannot be undone.

What stays

Data already stored locally on your own device is not affected by a server-side deletion request — if you also want your local data removed, uninstall the app or clear its storage from your device settings.

Timeframe

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.
